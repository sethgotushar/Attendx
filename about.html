ABOUT THE CREATOR

Hi, I'm Tushar.

I'm a student and developer interested in Python, web development, electronics and real-world technology projects.

AttendX was created around a simple problem: students often know that they need to maintain a minimum attendance percentage, but they don't always know how many days they can miss or how many days they need to attend.

The goal of AttendX is to turn that confusion into a simple, visual and understandable plan.
